01The problem

Every sensor sees the world differently.

Exposure, spectral response and bit depth change from camera to camera, and a detector trained on one camera's RAW stumbles on the next.

02The method

A global curve, then a local grid.

A light adapter in front of the detector, steered by the RAW histogram.

  1. 1Bézier curve

    One tone curve per channel, pinned to black and white.

  2. 2Bilateral grid

    A 3×3 colour matrix for every cell and brightness level.

  3. 3Histogram guidance

    A 64-quantile descriptor steers both, sensor by sensor.

  1. Linear RAWWhat the sensor records. At night, almost black.
  2. Bézier curveOne curve per channel lifts the tones.
  3. Bilateral gridColour is finished locally, cell by cell.
